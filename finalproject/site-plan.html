Site Name

- Dune Passage Runners Ica

This name represents a club for runners who live in the city of Ica, amd it also refers to the dunes because there is a desert with a lagoon near this city that is a well-known tourist destination.

Site Purpose

The site provides information on how the club is organized, the activities such as weekly practices and competitions, as well as a form where they can join the club.
